01  /  home

Who we are

Reaperotics began with a simple realisation: instead of searching for opportunities, we can build one.

We are a team of engineering students from different streams who believe real skill comes from building real things. We combine robotics, UAV systems, wireless communication, embedded systems, CAD and AI to create projects, learn in public and grow into a training and product company.

02  /  about

Vision & mission

To become one of India's most recognised names in engineering and innovation. We start in Visakhapatnam with robotics and intelligent systems, and we will grow far beyond them, building companies, products and people that the world will remember.

Mission 01

Build practical projects and products that solve real problems, and share what we learn openly.

Mission 02

Give every member a professional environment to learn, lead and be credited.

Mission 03

Make quality technical training accessible and affordable to students.
